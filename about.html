1. Company Overview & Introduction

IFEANYI CHIME AND CHIMA LTD. is a premier, multidisciplinary engineering, construction, and oil & gas support services conglomerate established in July 2016. As an indigenous Nigerian industrial powerhouse, we operate with a robust capital framework, delivering turn-key technical solutions across sovereign, public, and high-net-worth private sectors.

Our organization is strategically structured to manage complex assets from conceptual engineering design straight through to capital deployment, final execution, and life-cycle asset management, ensuring flawless compliance with international regulatory frameworks.

Operational Capabilities & Governance

Our enterprise framework coordinates multiple heavy disciplines, ensuring institutional oversight across all tiers of field delivery.

Technical Capabilities

Comprehensive macro project planning, structural front-end engineering design (FEED), heavy site management, rapid logistics deployment, and total quality assurance protocols.

Management Hierarchy

Led directly by the Managing Director/CEO, supported by individual divisions: Operations, Project Control, Technical Teams, Procurement Asset Units, and Safety Compliance Officers.

Industrial Resources & Asset Footprint

We maintain full ownership of heavy machinery fleets and specialized human engineering assets required to self-execute multi-tier tenders concurrently.

Heavy Operational Machinery Fleets

Excavators, Heavy Bulldozers, Industrial Dump Trucks, Multi-cubic Concrete Mixers, Structural Welding Systems, Sub-surface Dredgers, Ballast Barges, Offshore Support Vessels, and Heavy-lift Cranes.

Multi-Disciplinary Workforce

Certified Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical Engineers, Specialized Structural Welders, Industrial Fabricators, Heavy Machinery Technicians, Project Superintendents, and Dedicated HSE Field Officers.

Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Policy

Safety sits at the core of our industrial execution models. We enforce a zero-accident project culture, protecting field teams and assets while strictly conserving local coastal ecosystems and marine environments.

Quality Assurance (QA) Policy

We guarantee all structural solutions survive long-term environmental degradation. Our rigorous internal testing parameters follow premium international engineering methods and strict material controls.
